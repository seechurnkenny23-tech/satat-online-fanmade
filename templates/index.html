About this project

This is a fan-made browser implementation inspired by a traditional Mauritian card game. It is not presented as an official, endorsed, or affiliated release.

Rules in this version

  • 4 human players in fixed partnerships.
  • Trump is chosen by the player to the dealer's left.
  • The 2♥ is always the highest card and can be played on any trick.
  • You normally follow suit, except 2♥ can break that rule.
  • First team to 7 tricks wins the round.
